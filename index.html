How it works

One continuous line,
from room to reality.

  1. 01

    Measure

    A guided survey on the phone captures walls, windows, doors and utilities as you walk the room. Nothing to sketch, nothing to retype.

  2. 02

    Design

    An intelligent design engine lays out cabinets and appliances, and checks every clearance, door swing and work zone as it goes.

  3. 03

    See it

    Photoreal 3D, plans and elevations from one design, so the homeowner, the designer and the installer all see the same kitchen.

  4. 04

    Price & build

    Every line priced from the manufacturer's own catalog, and handed straight to the professional tools the shop already uses.
